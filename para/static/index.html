Before you add files

Are you allowed to use these documents with AI?

Many publishers' licences don't allow their documents to be copied into, or processed by, AI tools. That includes some technical standards (such as British Standards), warranty providers' technical manuals and subscription services. Your employer may have its own rules too.

Before adding a document, check its licence or terms of use, or ask whoever supplied it. Documents you add stay on this computer, but if you use an engine on another computer or a cloud service, passages from them are sent to it with your questions.
